# Step 11 User Acquisition Playbook

## Objective
Acquire the first real users from the locked ICP using one channel only, validate that they can complete the core workflow, and record why they do or do not get value.

## Locked ICP
- independent AI consultants delivering client work directly
- boutique agencies producing repeatable client deliverables with multiple LLM providers
- operators who already compare outputs manually or switch between providers during drafting/review

## Locked Use Case
Run the same client brief through multiple providers and reusable personas, compare outputs side by side, preserve the conversation history, and use analytics/admin diagnostics to improve repeatability over time.

## Channel Lock
Use founder-led direct outbound only.

Do not split effort across:
- ads
- content marketing
- community posting
- partnerships
- broad cold-email automation

## Real User Definition
A Step 11 user counts only if all of the following are true:
- they fit the locked ICP
- they had a direct conversation, demo, or onboarding exchange
- they received access and attempted the real workflow
- their outcome and objections were recorded

These do not count:
- friends doing casual clicks
- generic signups with no workflow attempt
- people outside the ICP
- AI hobbyists who only want free usage with no client-delivery workflow

## Target List Rules
Build an initial list of 30 to 50 prospects.

Each prospect must have:
- name
- company or solo practice name
- ICP fit reason
- service type or deliverable type
- contact path
- outreach status
- demo status
- onboarding status
- outcome

## Prospect Selection Criteria
Prioritize prospects who already do one or more of the following:
- create client strategy briefs
- compare model outputs manually in separate tabs
- produce repeatable deliverables such as outlines, reports, copy, proposals, or research summaries
- need reusable prompting styles across clients or projects

Disqualify prospects who:
- only want a generic chatbot
- are looking for full enterprise governance before even trying the workflow
- do not work with repeatable deliverables
- do not have any reason to compare providers or reuse personas

## Outreach Message
Use this exact structure:
- sentence 1: identify the workflow they likely already do
- sentence 2: explain the product in one line
- sentence 3: explain why it is better than swapping between ChatGPT/Claude tabs
- sentence 4: ask for a short test or demo

Recommended outreach copy:

"You look like exactly the kind of team that has to run the same client brief through more than one model and keep the best version. I built a workspace for that exact job: reusable personas, multi-provider runs, side-by-side comparison, and saved conversation history in one place. It is meant to replace the tab-hopping and copy-paste loop, not be another generic chatbot. If that is relevant, I want 15 minutes to walk you through it and watch where it helps or breaks."

## Demo Booking Goal
Initial batch:
- contact 10 prospects
- aim for 3 demos booked
- do not expand the batch until the first objections are reviewed

## Tracking Fields
Every outreach entry must record:
- prospect id
- name
- company
- role
- icp fit reason
- source
- first contact date
- reply status
- demo booked date
- demo completed date
- onboarding completed
- provider configured
- persona created
- comparison-ready conversation saved
- willing to use on client work
- top objection
- top friction point
- next action

## Manual Operating Cadence
Day 1:
- finalize the first 10 prospects
- send the first outreach batch

Day 2 to 4:
- run demos
- log objections immediately after each call
- tighten the message only if the first message is clearly missing the mark

Day 5:
- review outcomes
- decide whether the next batch should be another 10 prospects from the same channel

## Success Threshold For Step 11
Step 11 is only complete when all of the following are true:
- 10 real ICP users were reached, onboarded, or meaningfully tested
- at least 3 completed the core workflow
- top 5 objections are ranked
- top 5 friction points are ranked
- there is a clear answer to who actually wants the product and why

## What To Fix During Step 11
Allowed fixes:
- onboarding confusion
- provider setup friction
- persona setup confusion
- comparison/save flow blockers
- misleading copy on the first-run path

Not allowed:
- broad roadmap expansion
- unapproved scope cuts
- enterprise feature work
- generalized marketing experimentation
- adding a second ICP
